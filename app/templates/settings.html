🛡️ Cryptographic Architecture

Message Encryption: AES-256-GCM authenticated cipher with 12-byte random nonces and 128-bit authentication tags.

Configurable Fragmentation: Messages are divided into 2 to 10 parts. Plaintexts are reconstructed only after 100% of fragments pass integrity checks.

Shamir's Secret Sharing: Polynomial interpolation over prime field \(\mathbb{F}_p\) (\(p = 2^{256} + 297\)) dividing 256-bit encryption keys into threshold shares.

Database Backend: MongoDB Atlas cloud database storing user profiles, conversations, and ciphertext fragments.
